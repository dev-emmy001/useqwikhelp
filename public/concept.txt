Why does finding a reliable plumber in Nigeria feel like a gamble? Why does a 
skilled cook spend almost her week chasing word-of-mouth leads instead of 
doing more of the work she is actually good at? Why does someone pay a 
technician in cash, upfront, with no way to verify who he is or what happens if 
the job is wrong? 
These are not questions about technology. They are questions about trust. And 
the answer, in every case, is the same: there is no system. The Nigerian urban 
service economy, worth tens of billions of dollars annually, runs on personal 
referrals, WhatsApp messages, and hope. Almost nobody has built the 
infrastructure that makes these transactions safe, fair, and accountable for both 
sides. 
That is why QwikHelp exists. We do not exist because it seemed like a good 
market opportunity. We exist because the people in this market: the customer 
who gets burned, the provider who sometimes goes unpaid/underpaid, deserve 
better than what currently exists. 
Trust is the foundation of every transaction. QwikHelp exists to build that foundation 
for the Nigerian service economy 
The Problem 
The Nigerian informal service economy is large, active, and completely 
unstructured. Millions of transactions happen every day between households 
needing services and workers providing them, with no verified identity, no 
payment protection, and no accountability when things go wrong. 
Four Compounding Problems 
1.  No trusted discovery 
There is no structured way to find a service provider and verify who they are 
before allowing them into your home or business. The only filter is personal 
referral, which is unreliable, unscalable, and excludes providers who are 
genuinely skilled but poorly connected. 
2.  No payment protection 
Customers pay cash upfront with no recourse if the work is substandard or 
incomplete. Providers do the work and sometimes collect nothing, or less than 
agreed. Neither party has any financial protection in the transaction. 
3.  No dispute mechanism 
When something goes wrong: poor work, damage, non-payment, there is no 
formal process to resolve it. Disputes end in confrontation, social media 
complaints, or financial loss with no outcome. Both parties absorb the cost of 
having no system. 
4.  No provider income stability 
Skilled service workers in Nigeria have no platform to build a verifiable 
reputation, no guaranteed workflow, and no digital financial record of their 
earnings. Income is feast or famine, entirely dependent on personal networks 
that take years to build and can disappear overnight. 
The Solution  
QwikHelp provides the trust infrastructure that will make service transactions in 
Nigeria safe, fair, and accountable for both sides. It will not be a directory or a 
social platform: it will be a structured marketplace with verified identities, secure 
payments, in-app communication, and a formal dispute resolution system. 
How it works: Customer 
• Opens QwikHelp and searches for providers by proximity or posts a 
broadcast request. 
• Reviews verified profiles: real identity, service category, ratings, and job 
history. 
• Books and pays through the app. Payment is held securely until both 
parties confirm completion. 
• Communicates entirely in-app via text, voice notes, and calls. 
• Confirms job completion. A 24-hour window allows dispute submission if 
needed. 
• Funds released to the provider's wallet. Rating submitted. 
How it works:  Provider 
• Signs up and completes identity verification: NIN/BVN check, photo ID, 
face verification, and a Guarantor. 
• Admin reviews and approves the account. Profile goes live. 
• Toggles online and appears in proximity search results. 
• Receives job requests directly or responds to customer broadcast requests. 
• Sees payment confirmed in escrow before starting any work. 
• Completes job, confirms with customer, earnings credited to wallet 
instantly. 
• Withdraws to any Nigerian bank account on demand. 
Visibility on QwikHelp will be earned, not purchased. Provider ranking is 
determined entirely by rating, completion rate, and response time. No provider 
can pay for a higher position. This will keep the platform fair for providers and 
trustworthy for customers. 
The Product 
QwikHelp is a cross-platform mobile application built for iOS and Android. The 
product has two distinct user experiences: one for customers and one for 
providers, each designed for the specific needs and journey of that user type.
